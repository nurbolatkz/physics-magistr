English: aim for 35+

A compact lesson and 50 question quiz based on the grammar topics in english_format.doc.

How to study

Memorize the rules below, then answer one question at a time. After you press Check, read the explanation—even when you are correct.

Target: 35 correct out of 50. This practice score is a study target, not an official exam prediction.

Rules worth remembering first

1. Tense cluesEvery day → present simple; now → present continuous; yesterday → past simple; since/for → present perfect.
2. Finished vs connected pastUse past simple with a finished time. Use present perfect for experience or a result relevant now.
3. Articlesa/an = one nonspecific singular countable thing; the = specific/known; no article for general plural or uncountable nouns.
4. Countabilitymany/few + countable plural; much/little + uncountable; some/any work with both.
5. Modals and passiveModal + base verb; modal passive = modal + be + past participle. Ordinary passive = be + past participle.
6. ConditionalsIf + present, will + verb (real future). If + past, would + verb (imagined present). If + past perfect, would have + participle (imagined past).
7. Gerunds and infinitivesAfter a preposition use -ing. enjoy/avoid + -ing; want/decide + to + verb.
8. Prepositionsin months/years, on days/dates, at times; since = starting point, for = duration.
9. Comparisons and adverbsComparative + than; superlative + the. Adjectives describe nouns; adverbs describe actions.
10. Pronouns and relativeswho for people, which for things, whose for possession. Reflexive pronouns end in -self/-selves.
