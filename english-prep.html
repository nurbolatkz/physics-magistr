How to study

Memorize the rules below, then answer one question at a time. After you press Check, read the explanation—even when you are correct.

Target: 40 correct out of 50 (80%). The exceptions are where most points are lost, so open every card, read the Исключения list and do all its questions. Then run the Exceptions drill until you score 80%+. This practice score is a study target, not an official exam prediction.
